### Ranked, runoff and unknown-person outcomes

- A general election can resolve a winner, select runoff finalists or remain unresolved. Advancing to a runoff is not winning office. A later potential event must preserve its activation probability and whether its summaries are conditional on occurring; an inactive branch has no fabricated zero-turnout election.
- Ranked-ballot rounds, transfers and exhausted/inactive ballot categories are process details, separate from final winner summaries. Raw first choices or auxiliary plurality arrays cannot supply final winner probabilities.
- Preserve unresolved procedure, tied elimination/finalist boundary, no valid votes, unknown person identity and unresolved chamber alignment distinctly. A draw-local unidentified person cannot be displayed as one pooled candidate named Other, nor silently assigned a party.
- Bind every event/phase, candidate or unknown-person outcome, rule scenario, weighted summary and joint result. Conditional branch denominators must be explicit. No frontend kernel will tabulate ballots, invent a tie breaker or redistribute unresolved probability.

The current private count prototype covers plurality/tie/held-rule behavior; it does not yet represent ranked rounds or conditional runoff summaries. The owners have been asked for draft outcome/phase semantics and representative examples before extending this presentation model. No production controls or successor API fields are inferred from internal research variables.
