# Independent E01 aggregate results review

2026-10-25. This review is confined to the retrospective ordinary D/R Senate **F-only aggregate** experiment. It is not a full count-stack comparison, calibration-cut validation, current 2026 prediction, causal inflation estimate, or promotion decision. No sampler/process was restarted, stopped or launched; producer artifacts and runtime were read only.

Frozen execution plan: `prepared-v2/plan.json`, SHA-256 `e3faefeb89a5bca837bc7f8b01e666112d563b95fe16e909060edf5aa1cbfc50`. The launch binds that plan and runtime acceptance SHA `956a66375738bb5e2be2e0296e734c8e46ee7c94a90e3e19cc3618b27a63fcac`. The prior preparation/execution reviews remain applicable; the four arms are H0, F0, E01 and the predeclared E01_sd025 sensitivity. The sensitivity cannot be selected as an outcome-blind winning model.

The independent scripts in this directory do not import the runner or scoring module. `recompute.py` independently computes empirical CRPS using an order-statistic identity, WIS from central 80/95 intervals and median, winner Brier/log scores, coverage and widths, common forecast masks and original-order chain/batch Monte Carlo errors. It checks exact target support, prediction shape (4 chains × 20,000 future simulations × races), original 4,000 posterior-row/five-replicate mapping, raw-bank/prediction/seal pins, outcome labels against the pinned source, and source/plan/runtime bytes. Tiny CSV-parser final-ULP differences in source truth are allowed up to 2e-16; actual scoring differences have a separate 1e-10 ceiling. No race resampling or refit occurs.

`check_diagnostics.py` independently loads complete raw chains for 2020/2022/2024, verifies four chains, 4,000 retained and 1,500 warmup rows, and recomputes parameter rank Rhat, bulk/tail ESS, divergences and maximum-depth hits with the certified ArviZ runtime. Luna owns the complementary independent 2018 diagnostic review. Machine-readable recomputation/diagnostic receipts retain exact inspected SHA pins rather than a path-only assertion.

The seal promise is explicitly **four arms within each chronological target fold**, not all four historical folds before scoring. Frozen `execution_order` reads `chronological_fold; all_four_prediction_seals_and_F0_masks_before_target_score_labels`. The runner fits/seals H0/F0/E01/E01_sd025 for one cycle, freezes F0 masks, parses that cycle's numeric outcomes, then proceeds to the next cycle. Earlier evaluation labels legitimately train later folds. Having 2018/2020/2022 scores while 2024 still runs is therefore consistent. Code ordering and hash-bound artifacts support this account. Filesystem seal≤mask≤score timestamps are checked as consistency evidence only; they are not cryptographic proof of first label access or an unopened global holdout.

Competitive masks use F0 probability [.1,.9] and near-even [.4,.6] identically for every arm. Empty masks remain unavailable; notably 2022 has no near-even races, so an A-tier two-cycle near-even comparison is unavailable. Race means within cycles and equal-cycle A/B aggregation avoid weighting national evidence by race count. Numerical errors quantify finite chain/simulation integration, not uncertainty from two elections per tier; constant empirical winner tails remain unresolved even with zero delta-method MCSE. No general significance claim follows from a two-MCSE flag.

The source-vintage, final-roster and retrospective archive qualifications remain inherited. Target labels did not train their own fold or its scaler, but these are revisitable development outcomes. E01's macro coordinate strongly overlaps presidential party/national carryover and the 2022 CPI value extrapolates beyond earlier raw CPI support. Joint fitting is required to evaluate complementarity; a coefficient interpretation is not a causal effect and poor results do not prove inflation is irrelevant in every model. A new specification would require a newly versioned replay, not rewriting this negative record.

Positive differences are losses. E01's equal-cycle A CRPS loss is **+.001397508**, numerical MCSE .000067948 (about 4.2% of F0's A CRPS). The tighter sensitivity's A loss is **+.000354923**, MCSE .000055112. Both specifications worsen each A cycle; this is not merely a pooled loss caused by different race counts. E01 also worsens A WIS (+.000700599), Brier (+.003356585) and log score (+.011256338). Its A 80% intervals are wider on average by .005625617 share units while coverage falls by .016666667; 95% coverage is unchanged. The tighter prior reduces the damage but does not reverse it.

The competitive-mask A results point the same way. Primary E01 CRPS losses are +.000245275 in 2018 (11 races) and +.002472184 in 2022 (9 races); Brier also worsens. The near-even mask has only one race in 2018 and none in 2022, preventing a useful two-cycle A conclusion from that stratum. No empty-mask value was treated as zero.

Equal-cycle B CRPS differences are +.000045010 for E01 (numerical MCSE .000045171) and −.000025972 for the sensitivity (MCSE .000034782). These tiny net changes do not establish a useful transfer gain beyond integration noise; B WIS/Brier/log-score means worsen for both variants. A two-MCSE flag is only an integration diagnostic and not a national-election significance test. The stronger A losses cannot reasonably be explained by the reported numerical error alone, but four historical cycles still do not identify a universal causal inflation effect.

H0 remains a separate reference, not the nested macro control. F0 improves all-race CRPS over H0 substantially in 2020/2022/2024 and is almost tied in 2018, while some winner-score comparisons favor H0. This review therefore does not declare F0 universally dominant or rank a full stack's weakest layer. The causal/mechanistic question is narrower: adding this president-oriented headline-CPI mean to the existing relative-context aggregate law did not improve the target-context historical evidence.

**Disposition:** accept these results as computationally verified, bounded retrospective evidence and retain the negative outcome. Do not recommend promoting E01 or its tighter sensitivity over F0 on this experiment. The data do not justify a current-2026 CPI forecast adjustment, FP claim, full-count promotion or public activation. This is not a requirement that every metric/cycle must improve: both A cycles lose and no substantive B offset is demonstrated. Testing another economic mechanism or adapting priors after seeing these results would be a new exposed-development version, not an outcome-blind rescue of this run.
