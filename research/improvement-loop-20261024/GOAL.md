# Continuous model improvement

Standing goal authorized by Hayden, October 24, 2026. Completing one comparison does not complete this goal.

Continuously improve Ballotmancy's modular forecasting stack through reproducible research, independent verification and fair model comparisons. Build a durable, resumable process capable of thousands of improvement cycles.

## First, verify the existing stack

Inventory all eight layers: population/geography, community composition, fundamentals, national environment, measurement, participation, candidate choice, and counts/election rules. For this initial inventory, an existing research implementation counts as present, even if unpublished. Preserve those implementations; fill genuinely missing layers with established, independently checked methods.

Verify that every layer performs meaningful work and that the layers function coherently together. Check shared uncertainty, compatible quantities, joint inference, evidence ownership and interactions. Distinguish implementation correctness, successful fitting, predictive usefulness and publication readiness.

## Then investigate what is missing

Use recursive Luna and Sol research rounds to identify overlooked mechanisms, data and model components. Have agents challenge each other's findings, check existing implementations for overlap, verify consequential claims against primary sources, and refine promising hypotheses into concrete experiments. Distinguish a missing mechanism from missing data or an inadequately tested existing model.

## Establish and repeat the improvement loop

1. Identify the weakest supported layer by its incremental contribution to the complete forecast, including interactions, uncertainty and computational reliability. Mark insufficient evidence explicitly.
2. Research its failures and plausible remedies recursively with Luna and Sol. Prefer a strong, established method before introducing novelty.
3. Develop a versioned challenger while preserving the incumbent. Jointly refit affected components when required.
4. Compare incumbent and challenger on matched data, cutoffs, race coverage, forecast horizons and tuning opportunities. Apply the project's refine-model-layer and verify-election-model skills. Evaluate both recent same-context and other-context elections, proper predictive scores, calibration, useful precision, and relevant count or chamber outcomes.
5. Independently review the implementation and results. Investigate material regressions and distinguish genuine improvement from simulation noise, data advantages or repeated benchmark tuning.
6. Promote a qualified winner to the layer's active incumbent. If evidence is inconclusive or the challenger underperforms, retain the incumbent. Keep losing models, historical results, presets and saved user configurations available. Promotions must be reversible and must not silently rewrite saved selections.
7. Integrate, test and publish qualified changes, reassess the stack, and select the next weakest layer.

Revise one layer at a time, with explicit interaction experiments where needed. A layer replacement may require a new jointly fitted full configuration; do not splice incompatible fitted components together. Maintain versioned sources, configurations, predictions, comparisons, decisions, negative findings and resumable checkpoints. Reuse prior research and avoid repeating unsuccessful experiments without a new reason.

Continue autonomously across cycles. Coordinate with the Senate and House owners, preserve their work, and advance independent work while dependencies are pending. Report genuine blockers precisely and preserve everything needed to resume. Do not claim that an unattended scheduler, a successful fit, empirical qualification or publication exists before it does.

## Meaning of active

The initial inventory uses **implemented in research**. Later promotion means the **selected incumbent for an explicit scientific scope and complete compatible configuration**. Public availability is a separate, tested integration decision. These three meanings must remain visible and must not be inferred from one another.

The user's phrase "without deleting the user" is interpreted here as preserving both the losing model and users' saved configurations. No destructive cleanup is part of this goal.
