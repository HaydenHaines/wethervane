**No CRPS sign defect exists in the original draft.** Its upper influence is `−2*SF*(F_t−F)`, correctly equivalent to `+2*SF*(SF_t−SF)` because `SF_t−SF=−(F_t−F)`. The latter positive-SF-difference form is also correct for Root's helper. A negative sign on an SF-difference would be wrong, but that is not what this unchanged draft says. The earlier concern was a reading error, not a draft repair. WIS's50/80/95 denominator3.5 is correct.

The incoming-row/incoming-lambda Gaussian record and outgoing-row/post-gamma record describe distinct valid phases. Observed-race CDFs use the Gaussian conditional computed for the **incoming** row/lambda; do not relabel it with the returned outgoing row. Missing-race Student-t marginals use the outgoing row and retained cycle. Column squared norms of `L^-1` give `diag(J^-1)` for `J=LLᵀ`; row norms would be wrong. Equal averaging of retained conditional records is correct Rao–Blackwellization. Posterior row-weight accumulators are diagnostics, **not additional weights applied again to those already posterior-distributed records**. All16,000 original parameter rows remain in the actual polling prior; archived F0 reuse does not change that.

Root's proposed **archived-only `F0_archived_predictive` reference is acceptable** and avoids unnecessary nested per-row cycle quadrature. Freeze exact source/law/feature/race equality, archive bytes, generalized-inverse quantiles and the20–80% competitive/40–60% near-even masks before labels. Empirical CRPS/Brier/WIS are proper scores for that explicitly declared empirical predictive distribution. They are not numerically exact summaries of the underlying continuous finite-bank F0 law. Preserve all four source chains,4,000 joint rows and five contiguous future replicates; do not call80,000 draws independent parameter rows or silently inherit old score/mask conventions.

I have **no substantive objection** to F0 WIS as a descriptive exact empirical-reference score with only source-chain-specific WIS dispersion and no certified nonlinear MCSE. An empirical CDF has no smooth density for the continuous quantile-influence formula. Do not manufacture that density, add a resampling platform, or certify an FS-versus-F0 WIS difference from that dispersion. This does not invalidate separately qualified primary **FS6−FS5** WIS on the common fixed mask. Required-control presence need not mean every control's approximation uncertainty is certified identically; state that distinction in the disposition. Row-clustered CRPS/Brier influence remains useful, with explicit distinction between uncertainty relative to underlying F0 and scores conditional on fixed archive bytes. Empirical constant winner tails remain unresolved for population log probabilities, not smoothed into qualification.

**Metric-specific admission is scientifically preferable to whole-forecast rejection on any tiny tail.** Freeze per-side tail statuses before labels, separately from core state convergence, bounded win/share precision, interval-display precision and inherited-bank warnings. After labels, each log score can use only its corresponding precomputed outcome tail; FS5/FS6 paired qualification requires both arms' corresponding tail. Retain every fixed-mask row/status. If any required realized-outcome tail is unresolved, the full-mask aggregate log score is unavailable: do not preferentially average the surviving realized outcomes, since that can omit surprising rare losses even without post-label threshold tuning. No primary promotion with an unresolved required score; other precise descriptive scores remain usable. No labels-triggered sampling, changed mask or clipped primary score.

Root's selected retry policy is appropriate: one predeclared larger run for all three polling arms in a fold on core state/Rhat/mean-ESS, bulk mean-share/win absolute precision or bank-chain consistency failure. Tiny-tail, interval-display, inherited-bank concentration and post-label score failures should not independently trigger wholesale repeated sampling. Preserve both attempts and the fixed selected-attempt rule. Relative-tail≤5% and chain log-tail agreement .15 are numerical checks **conditional on frozen bank/calibration bytes**, not scientific tail guarantees. They can fail for extremely small unused tails without invalidating precise Brier/share quantities.

Separate the .005 total3-MCSE quantile-bracket display rule from primary score admission. As an illustrative iid-indicator calculation, q=.025 and share density.6 need about97,500 effective observations for a total±3-MCSE bracket width.005. Pointwise estimated3-MCSE brackets are not simultaneous coverage guarantees.

The continuous-mixture influence mathematics is otherwise appropriate: Brier/log influences, mixture quantiles rather than averaged component quantiles, transformed-share densities, and averaging race influences **before** chain/batch MCSE preserve common-cycle dependence. Log-tail derivatives should use scaled component/mixture ratios, not underflowing probabilities. Independent arm streams justify addition of conditional numerical variances, not a paired-draw claim. Tier numerical variances are conditional on fixed bank/source bytes; they exclude inherited historical MCMC, calibration uncertainty and election-to-election variation. Per-chain nonlinear score recomputation is a diagnostic of linearization, not an independent four-election error estimate.

Vectorize/chunk shared Gaussian conditional CDF evaluations, validated fixed/vectorized conditional expectations or shared quadrature nodes, retaining chain/batch functional sums as needed. Avoid storing state×race×quadrature-node tensors. Archived-only F0 removes the most expensive16,000-row nested integration branch. Gaussian-only helper qualification does not automatically qualify Student-t missing-race marginal arithmetic: keep the common-polled primary routines within their qualified scope and qualify any extra full-bank branch separately.

Inherited-bank concentration thresholds are sensible warning flags rather than proof that the finite-bank posterior is invalid. `1/sum(wbar²)` is concentration, not original HMC ESS; extra Gibbs draws cannot replace absent historical support. Flag source-chain domination and integration consistency without imposing equal posterior source-chain mass. No automatic original-row bootstrap/refit follows.
