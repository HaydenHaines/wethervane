Core conclusion supported: the official2018/2020 November Voting documentation offers an approximate marginal-SE route, so a public replicate file is not logically required for every uncertainty calculation. This does not admit a joint registration/voting likelihood. Primary PDFs inspected independently: https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cps/techdocs/cpsnov18.pdf and https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cps/techdocs/cpsnov20.pdf . Formulas were checked against rendered pages and numerical illustrations, not merely garbled extraction. Totals use sqrt(a*x²+b*x). Percentage SE uses sqrt((b/y)*p*(100−p)), with p in percentage points and y the estimated population base; it is not a formula for an arbitrary ratio. Difference formula sums marginal variances under the documented approximation. The warning about positive correlation making that difference-SE approximation too large, and negative correlation too small, is correct.

**Material clarifications.** The initially displayed draft equated PES2 edited-universe inclusion of PES1=-9 with being asked the question. By the time the exact reviewed snapshot was captured (addendum SHA8c853397...), Luna had corrected that wording: the snapshot explicitly separates edited universes from questionnaire routes and notes no separate no-response route is shown. This correction resolves the identified routing defect. Do not infer actual asking/answering/nonresponse coverage or registration status from the edited code list.

Geographic implementation also needs explicit documentation:2018PDF pp198–199 and2020pp204–205(1-based), plus their subsequent tables, distinguish Tables9–11 total/White voting/registration parameters for state/division/region from Table8 national parameters. Other population groups use appropriate geographic factors from Tables12–14 applied to both a,b. The draft's general geography caveat is directionally correct but its Table8-centric citations omit these operational instructions. A state/demographic adapter cannot use national Table8 coefficients without the prescribed mapping. Availability of a geographic identifier does not qualify arbitrary fine-area uncertainty. Fine intersections still need support checks, not automatic independence.

Nonsampling wording should remain careful: the documentation says some nonsampling error can enter SE estimation, but these functions are not a full uncertainty budget or a correction for systematic reporting, coverage, imputation/nonresponse bias.2020PDF pp196–197 explicitly distinguishes household and person nonresponse rates and warns against combining them. Do not turn marginal GVF variance into all measurement-error variance, or infer absence of bias from a small SE. No such numerical bias correction was demonstrated.

**Covariance boundary.** The difference approximation is not an identified joint covariance matrix across overlapping margins, cells or repeated releases. Choosing independent Gaussian observations with those SEs would add a working dependence assumption, not reproduce a Census-endorsed joint likelihood. Complements and sums create constraints; total/percentage observations from the same people must not be treated as new independent evidence. A GVF based on direct replicate variances does not expose the underlying public replicate family, its person join or original vintage. These distinctions in the addendum are sound.

**Feasible next research.** A bounded documentary/arithmetic gate can first freeze a few explicitly supported national/state aggregate estimands, each numerator/base/characteristic/race/geography coefficient and table locator, then reproduce the official worked SE examples and unit/percentage conversion checks. It can reject unsupported cells, negative radicands and denominator/scale errors. Do not silently clip invalid variance to zero. No actual dataset or outcome need be opened for this initial test. A subsequent source-admitted marginal comparison may use those approximate SEs with explicit working-law/dependence and bias limitations; it must not claim design covariance or stage identification. Historical first-release/revision proof and forecast-time cutoff checks remain separate gates. The current PDFs do not establish exact historical raw-file availability.

Verdict: retain the core practical correction, retain the corrected edited-universe/routing language and expand state/geographic table mapping.
