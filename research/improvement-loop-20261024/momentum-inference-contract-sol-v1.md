This Rao–Blackwell estimator integrates Gaussian state noise conditional on k/lambda, but still has Gibbs integration error from k/lambda.

`IS_alpha=(u-l)+(2/alpha)*(l-y)*1[y<l]+(2/alpha)*(y-u)*1[y>u]`;

`WIS=(0.5*abs(y-m)+sum_alpha (alpha/2)*IS_alpha)/3.5`.

K=3, denominator K+0.5=3.5. Endpoints inclusive for coverage; equality gives zero miss penalty. This is the corrected standard convention of [Bracher et al.](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1008618) and its [published correction](https://journals.plos.org/ploscompbiol/article?id=10.1371/journal.pcbi.1010592).
