# G01 aggregate gasoline preparation

`prepared-v2/plan.json` (SHA-256 `ee3cdefc9b3a5f9bc98598acd47ef7db81d0393f32c874396843e587566849ef`) prepares H0, F0 and G01 for four retrospective ordinary Senate D/(D+R) folds. F0 to G01 is the primary comparison; H0 is descriptive. No sampler is imported or run by this package. The earlier `prepared-v1` is preserved and superseded as explained in `SUPERSESSION.md`.

The first operation pins the root G01 selection. The source join verifies the 32-file TWIP receipt inventory and checks national regular-retail units, official raw-source hashes, method regime, the exact reviewed 2004 header exception, positive prices, Monday observations exactly 91 days apart and log arithmetic. Both conservative source-availability upper bounds must precede election day minus fourteen days at **00:00 UTC**. The label-free Senate panel's own election date and cutoff must agree with that instant.

One TWIP observation exists for each even-year cycle from 2002 through 2024. The first two older Senate cycles, 1998 and 2000, have no gas row, so **all three arms** use the same 2002+ support. Training race counts are 236, 266, 294 and 324; targets have 30, 28, 30 and 30 races. National gas variation has only 8, 9, 10 and 11 earlier cycle observations respectively. The feature is the president-party-oriented 13-week natural log gasoline price change, centered and scaled by earlier unique cycles with equal cycle weights. G01 adds one sign-unrestricted Normal(0,.05) coefficient to F0's aggregate logit mean. Other F0 coefficients and scales are inferred jointly in a future fit. A zero G01 coefficient recovers F0's normalized density times the added proper prior density.

The plan includes outcome-blind prior predictive checks and the distinct raw-first centering diagnostic. Dated archived TWIP issues are accepted for retrospective development; original posting logs and no-revision history are not authenticated. The 2018 gasoline sample-method break remains visible, and no selected 91-day pair crosses it. This does not qualify a population count fit, 2026 forecast or public model.
