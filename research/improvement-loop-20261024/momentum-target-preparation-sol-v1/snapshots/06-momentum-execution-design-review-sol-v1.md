## Actual update law and joint uncertainty

`polling_basis_v1.py:190–265` implements blocked Gibbs for a finite empirical historical prior: conditionally draw observed race logits and shared target-cycle effect, update one categorical historical-row index, then update Student-t race precisions. These are sequential exact conditional transitions targeting one joint distribution, not a single Gaussian likelihood evaluated directly on beta. Every row change brings its complete original coefficients/tau-cycle/tau-race vector; revisiting rows is valid. The row factor at lines 148–187 includes row-dependent scale normalizers and the shared-cycle/race quadratic terms. Its constants independent of the row can cancel; one must not drop the row-dependent normalizers or reapply the historical coefficient prior as another factor.

The returned `posterior_chain`/`posterior_draw` map the selected row to source identity, distinct from the Gibbs chain/draw coordinates. New target-cycle effects are not old historical `cycle_z` effects. Unobserved race effects are generated conditional on the same selected row/shared cycle. Initial empirical row mass is uniform; its posterior mass changes. This is the declared marginal empirical-prior update, not a calibration cut or a guarantee of equal posterior bank weights. Covariance scales remain conditional MAP values. Their posterior uncertainty and their dependence with the fundamentals fit through shared historical outcomes are not integrated. The existing F0 bank can be held fixed identically across kernel arms; changing its law/source would instead require a separately versioned jointly refitted fundamentals bank.

The v2 corrections to the previous row-resampling prohibition, six-column F0 basis, and learned “no fundamentals” prior are appropriate. Existing updater compatibility is computational qualification, not empirical forecast qualification.

## Required diagnostic and provenance corrections

1. **Remove BFMI as a Gibbs gate.** The updater uses Gaussian/Gamma/categorical conditional draws, without Hamiltonian momenta, trajectories or energy state. BFMI measures HMC momentum-refresh/energy behavior; applying it to arbitrary negative log densities or row indices would not create an HMC diagnostic. Mark it not applicable with reason. See the primary [ArviZ BFMI documentation](https://python.arviz.org/projects/stats/en/v1.2.0/api/generated/arviz_stats.bfmi.html), which defines its momentum/energy purpose. Divergences/tree depth are likewise not Gibbs diagnostics.
2. **Match diagnostics to saved quantities.** Current returns include logits/shares, coefficient/scales, shared cycle, row/source identities, but **not** the precision-mixture `lam` trace. The plan cannot promise Rhat/ESS of that trace from existing outputs. Minimal choice: use the existing returned-state diagnostics and explicitly say internal precision trajectories are not retained. If Root requires those traces, implement a separate observation-only instrumentation contract, retain the original transition/RNG stream, and independently verify all original outputs before relying on it. Do not silently claim the unmodified operator supplies them.
3. **Do not use numeric row-index Rhat/ESS as a primary convergence certificate.** Row IDs are categorical labels; relabeling can change this statistic without changing the chain law. Diagnose meaningful coefficient/tau/cycle/logit/share/win functions, cross-chain row/source occupancy and concentration, plus repeated-chain numerical integration errors. Distinguish occupied-bank-row concentration from correlated-chain ESS, and inherited historical-bank error from new Gibbs error. Posterior source-chain occupancy need not be uniform. Constant empirical winner tails remain unresolved, not certified by zero MCSE.
4. **Fix two provenance sentences in a versioned correction/Root contract.** The shared canonical calibration input identity is `32563945a4cfd608efc68ed8f20600e4310705ef3ee736f1a378bf1372e8884a`; `f5201d12…` is the preparation **plan** SHA. Also the 31 Texas 2020 calibration rows are retained for the **2022 and 2024 target folds**, not the 2020 fold. The latter trains only through 2018. The table itself has the right cycles, so this is a contradictory prose statement rather than evidence of a source error. Preserve v2 rather than editing away its history.

The existing `posterior_diagnostics` at lines 267–308 reports multiscale batch/chain MCSE, rank/folded Rhat and mean-ESS estimates on actual returned states. It is not automatically identical to ArviZ bulk/tail ESS. A future numeric gate must name its estimator/coordinate set rather than calling every diagnostic “ESS.” Add per-race winner MCSE and score-difference MCSE, and declare how unresolved constant tails/interval precision are reported. Failed or inconclusive computation must remain visible and cannot become a score-based model rejection or win.

## Poll-only comparator

The fixed independent `Normal(0,2.5²)` final-logit prior is a coherent broad proper **reference** with no learned outcome-mean coefficients. Measurement-error calibration still uses historical outcomes; it is not an entirely history-free method. A different prior makes poll-only versus F0-plus-polls an informative benchmark, not a causal decomposition or clean test of only the slope mechanism.

Let H map race logits to polls, y be supplied poll logits and R the complete calibrated covariance. Its Gaussian posterior has `C=(I/2.5² + HᵀR⁻¹H)⁻¹`, `m=C HᵀR⁻¹y`. Repeated polls are correlated through R and are not separate inverse-variance independent measurements. Each marginal winner probability is exactly `Phi(m_r/sqrt(C_rr))`; share quantiles are the logistic transform of Gaussian quantiles. Expected shares/CRPS can use deterministic one-dimensional marginal integration with explicit tolerance. Joint covariance must remain available if joint outputs are ever requested, but a new multidimensional numerical integration framework is unnecessary for these racewise scores. Avoid identifying `logistic(m)` with expected share, or an unpolled prior prediction with observed polling evidence.

This comparator is optional for the first execution. If included, freeze its prior, exact mask, stable tail/log-score implementation and integration tolerance before scores. Do not tune its width after comparing cycles. The clean mechanism contrast remains matched F0-plus-poll M01 versus M02.

## Operator fairness, admission and minimal choices

The proposal correctly separates latest-per-race/pollster from the native full series. Latest destroys repeated named-pollster target contrasts; full series restores them but also changes the observation set and unverified sample/response-overlap assumptions. To estimate both kernel and data-operator effects, use the **2×2** Brownian/slope × latest/full-series design, with identical row IDs within each kernel pair. Do not call a full-series M02 versus latest M01 difference a kernel improvement. A complete 2×2 is required only if the report claims operator interaction/robustness, not as an automatic prerequisite to any narrower matched experiment.

My minimal first forecast proposal for Root is: **H14, native extra-lag0, one explicitly admitted operator, F0-only reference plus matched combined M01/M02**, all four A/B cycles. Full series is the mechanism-relevant candidate because it has repeated-pollster timing; choosing it still needs explicit admission and covariance assumptions. If Root wants a selected-latest anchor, add latest M01 as a separately labeled data-adapter reference, without claiming the missing M02/latest interaction. Alternatively freeze the full H14 2×2 (16 combined Gibbs jobs) and defer every other sensitivity. Do not simultaneously add H7, lag grids, all prior widths and both poll-only variants to the first bounded run.

Keep H30 excluded absent target-feature admission. H7, if later selected, is a stale-H14-feature poll cutoff sensitivity. Historical calibration remains the inclusive0..100-day fixed-bin selector, not a target-horizon truncation; three unique calibration inputs support four folds and do not acquire a 2022 calibration cycle merely because 2024 is evaluated. Root's separate calibration execution design owns optimizer gates and fit reuse; this forecast proposal does not supersede it.

No identity proposals become admissions by appearing in this design. Freeze exact event/candidate/question IDs, source hashes, cutoff/proxy arithmetic and outcome identity mask before forecasts. The unresolved 2022 CA event remains excluded; regular/special mappings need their reviewed receipt. Report source/roster/bank gaps and prior-only races rather than zero-filled polls. Full-bank F0 and common poll-supported comparisons need visibly different denominators. Native LV/RV/adult question priority is not the descriptive audit's LV/RV-only population universe. Advertised long/overlapping field periods are neither daily interview weights nor proof of respondent independence; no interval likelihood is currently admitted.
