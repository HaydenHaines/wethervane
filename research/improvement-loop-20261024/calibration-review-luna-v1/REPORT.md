The combined NetCDF equals the concatenated native chain files across posterior, warmup, and sampler-statistic groups.

The saved posterior bank has shape `(4 chains, 1000 draws, 5 ordered components)`, ordered independent, cycle, race, house, movement. Its log-scale array is exactly equal row by row to the post-warmup NetCDF `log_scales`; standard deviations match `exp(log_scales)` within a tight floating-point tolerance. Thus each row retains the **joint five-scale draw**; this is not an independently assembled product of five marginal samples. The observed posterior correlation matrix includes, for example, independent–house −0.356, independent–movement −0.299, and race–movement −0.391. Other pairwise correlations are near zero in this finite bank; no assumption that every pair must be correlated is used.

The frozen plan and launch record agree on the sampler parameters and four declared chain seeds. The pinned sampler source uses those per-chain seeds and the global seed to generate its fixed initial offsets. The journals' first callback states equal the values generated by that initial-offset formula for all four chains; because callbacks are post-transition, that observed equality is consistent with a rejected first transition and is not independent proof of the live process's random-seed consumption. The source and plan bind the declared seed configuration; the retained output binds the resulting draws.
