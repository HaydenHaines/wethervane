**Weights and uncertainty.** Existing CES source modules preserve/audit publisher weights; they do not supply a CPS design adapter. The selected strict survey row contract has no registration, weights or replicate matrix, and its raw-record survey factor is a sum of unweighted log probabilities. The conditional CES Dirichlet-multinomial utility conditions on supplied theta/n and does not model survey inclusion. The community-state model's exponential row-weight bootstrap is not independently validated as the CPS sampling design. Do not relabel either as official CPS design uncertainty.

For a summary observation law, independently acquire person replicate weights matched to the exact person file/version, exact join instructions and release availability, then predeclare domain estimates and the official replicate procedure. Recompute nonlinear ratios for each replicate; preserve joint covariance of registration, voting, overlapping demographics/states and denominators. Complements/sum constraints can make covariance singular: choose declared independent contrasts or a support-aware law, not independent binomials for every margin. Sparse/zero-support domains and missing replicate joins must fail or be explicitly unsupported. Design variance concerns sampling, not all misreport, proxy, nonresponse or cross-source bias. Separate those assumptions. A pseudoposterior is a distinct declared target requiring uncertainty justification, not automatically a normalized raw-record model.

check_weight_scale.py/result.json is an exact-rational invented warning: multiplying both persons' weights by 100 leaves their weighted mean 1/2 unchanged but divides a falsely constructed sum-of-weights binomial variance by 100. It demonstrates why publisher weighted population totals are not automatically sample sizes. It does not calculate actual CPS variance or validate a replacement likelihood.

Replicates estimate uncertainty, not new people. EAVS state totals plus the same summed jurisdictions are one evidence source, and old/new report likelihoods must not both consume the same total. Shared population calibration benchmarks or repeated CPS households across cycles may induce dependence; quantify or state limits rather than infer cross-cycle independence. Keep source adapters and factor-ownership manifests inspectable.
