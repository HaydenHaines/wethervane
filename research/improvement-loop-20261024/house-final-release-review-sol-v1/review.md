# Independent scientific review of the 2026 House release-v6 packet

26 October 2026. Scope: read-only authentication of selection, numerical/audit/binding, regression, source-policy and native-export metadata; structural inspection of all 16 exported results. The companion `pins.json` gives exact hashes for the selected receipts, audits, bindings, results and top-level packet files. I did not rerun fits, re-audit large raw banks or decode native joint archives; the website owner retains those import and deployment checks.

## Decision

**The scientific packet supports publishing this exact, dated, experimental 2026 House aggregate comparison if the website owner completes the native-archive import, operator review, actual-data browser checks, deployment and served-site verification.** It does not support a blanket model promotion or a claim that the default polling model is empirically superior for 2026. The source receipt explicitly says `scientific_packet_ready_for_website_review`, `publication_qualified: false`.

## Input and display scope

- Each native result has 435 unique regular House race and district IDs; all district IDs match the 435 features of the pinned 2026 GeoJSON. The map uses Census 2026 CD120 except Missouri's documented 2024 CD119 exception. Each has 412 conditional principal-pair shares. The 16 results agree on the roster, source provenance and 2026 map vintage. The roster includes 1,208 captured candidate records and documented declared write-ins, with 358 election-authority fields and 77 reviewed secondary fields; this is a dated captured field, not a guarantee against later filing or legal changes.
- The forecast cutoff is **2026-10-25 15:48:30 UTC**, with poll selection through **15:36:34 UTC**. `strict_as_of` is false because poll availability uses a declared ingestion proxy rather than verified original publication times. Every polling configuration has 167 selected question observations across 98 districts, from 61 pollster keys; no-poll configurations have zero. Shared source samples must not be described as 167 independent surveys. The selected polling set is not an exhaustive census, and 27 observations lack nominal sample size.
- The default result's D/R/O expected seats are 232.532/202.111/0.356. Its 218-seat **party-majority** probabilities are D 0.805173, R 0.186641, unresolved 0.008186. Other-party or independent winners stay unassigned; unresolved does not model Speaker negotiations. These are model outputs, not verified outcomes or empirical skill.
- Candidate/group win probabilities and conditional two-principal shares have different meanings. Do not attribute a pooled O or same-party group probability to a member. The Alaska and CA-06 independent-candidate predictor mappings are explicit proxies with unqualified transfer. Ranked-choice and runoff contests approximate ultimate winners without transfers, rounds, turnout or candidate counts. A House tipping probability is not qualified.

## Empirical limits and publication disclosure

The 2026 result has no observed outcome and no 2026 skill score. Historical A cycles are 2018/2022; B cycles are 2020/2024. The preserved retrospective work found polling gains in many comparisons but material 2020 share-score regressions and failed historical chamber precision. For example, the four historical implementations' 2020 all-eligible mean FP-minus-F CRPS is **+0.00237** (worse; from the preserved matched score CSV). Current simulation precision cannot erase that finding, measure source completeness, or establish empirical calibration. Its Monte Carlo error is conditional on finite fitted banks and plug-in polling-error scales. Label the release as a dated experimental aggregate forecast, show the model and poll cutoffs, preserve the historical caveat and link to methods/sources. Publish derived forecast facts, citation URLs and hashes; source-policy review reserves raw third-party bytes internally.

No scientific metadata blocker was found for this narrowly described packet. The remaining publication gates are website-owned and are listed in the release receipt, not satisfied by this review.
