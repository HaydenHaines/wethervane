# Independent M02 momentum challenger design review

2026-10-25. Read-only review of `momentum-challenger-plan-luna-v1.md`, the selected polling source/law and momentum audit v2. No fit, code change, source admission or frozen artifact change. **Disposition: accept for a bounded no-fit coverage/analytic prototype, with the execution-contract clarifications below required before forecasting fits.** The proposal does not yet authorize an executable historical comparison, a 2026 adjustment or promotion. No evidence establishes polling as the weakest layer.

The random-slope mechanism is a defensible small candidate, not a result derived from the 12 descriptive momentum cases. A zero-centered race slope with covariance `tau_slope² x xᵀ`, `x=days_before_election/30`, yields coherent positive-semidefinite within-race dependence and can infer either direction after observing polls. With the fixed positive independent-noise component the full covariance is positive definite. The slope is anchored at zero on Election Day: positive slope means earlier support is higher, not a positive election-day momentum bonus. Nothing about a rising candidate mechanically requires an upward adjustment.

## Exact incumbent and competing ownership

Verified `src/forecasting/poll_covariance_v1.py:21–61`: independent total noise, shared cycle bias, race-persistent bias, within-cycle house bias and Brownian movement `same_race*min(d_i,d_j)/30`. The existing independent term already owns sampling/weighting/rounding error; do not add nominal-N/MOE again. `gaussian_error_objective` at lines 79–100 fits the complete normalized Gaussian residual likelihood with a regularizer in log-SD coordinates. The selected source snapshot `research/polling-layer-implementation-20261020/forecast-v2/source-snapshots/01-standard-plan-root-v1.json` explicitly declares conditional MAP scales and unintegrated dependence with the fundamentals fit's common historical outcomes.

Brownian movement and the new rank-one kernel are mathematically distinct but can explain much of the same data. At one time point per race, the slope is indistinguishable from additional persistent race variance; at a narrow time span it is nearly so. Varying pollster composition can mimic either trend. Refitting all five existing covariance scales plus the sixth slope scale, under identical earlier-only rows and regularizers, is essential. Freezing the Brownian scale while adding slope would not isolate a mechanism. The plan correctly requires the joint six-scale refit; this is conditional empirical Bayes, not full posterior integration over scales.

Require at least a baseline-versus-augmented kernel condition/identifiability diagnostic, multi-start solution checks and the objective Hessian/profile or another explicitly defined uncertainty diagnostic. A MAP fit alone does not supply a posterior correlation between slope and Brownian scales: the plan's requested “correlation” needs an operational definition. Preserve a prior-dominated or inseparable result as inconclusive, rather than demanding a particular nonzero slope. The zero-slope **variance** limit reproduces the five-term likelihood; a finite lognormal regularizer excludes an exact zero scale, so distinguish a nested likelihood boundary from an attainable MAP parameter value. No scientific prior is obtained merely by copying an optimizer box.

## Execution-blocking selection clarification

The selected standard plan has two different operators: historical training takes one latest observation per cycle/race/pollster/fixed 14-day lead bin; its current forecast takes the latest sample per race/pollster and one question per sample. The challenger proposal alternates between the “full eligible poll series” and preserving the latest-sample convention. These are not interchangeable. Latest-per-pollster removes repeated within-pollster target contrasts, leaving cross-pollster time contrasts especially vulnerable to house/time confounding.

Before fitting, freeze the exact target operator and complete observation IDs. A matched M01/M02 comparison can use the same expanded historical-series adapter, but then name that adapter change explicitly and retain a selected-operator baseline if it materially changes evidence. Do not silently give only M02 more polls, concatenate sources, or call changed data selection the exact selected forecast. The no-fit coverage audit must report repeated same-pollster samples **after** availability/deduplication, time-span distributions, interval overlap, distinct time bins and number of eligible races, not merely raw answer-row counts. Specify a common-support inclusion rule before reading new scores, and retain the full admitted denominator/exclusion ledger. The existing v2 audit's single matched-pollster ≥1 pp case is a warning about support, not proof that every longer-horizon random-slope design is unidentified.

## Field intervals and availability

Keeping field-end age in both primary arms isolates the kernel change. Uniform integration over an advertised field interval is a sensitivity assumption, not known interview weighting. Ninety-day ActiVote metadata must not be expanded into 90 independently observed daily polls or interpreted as verified daily respondent allocation. Establish whether a reported range is a true field period, a rolling accumulation window, or another metadata convention; unsupported types stay explicitly excluded/unknown in the interval sensitivity.

The formula `E[min(D_i,D_j)]` with independent uniform dates represents a covariance of **uniformly averaged latent trajectories**, with a double integral even on the diagonal. It is not generally the Gaussian marginal law for one observation at an unknown random interview time. That latter construction is a mixture over date-dependent means/covariances; replacing it with an expected covariance changes the likelihood. Declare the average-observation operator, or label an expected-covariance approximation and test it. The random-slope average uses the interval mean coordinate coherently under that declared operator. Neither interpretation authenticates respondent interview dates, repeated-panel independence or original publication times.

The source proxies and 1/3/7-day lag sensitivities are appropriately explicit. Define whether lag applies relative to field end, recorded creation, or their maximum for each source, then filter before question/sample choice. Do not treat identical numerical lag values as identical source-age information across the Tierney author panel and 538. State `strict_asof_qualified:false`; fieldwork ending before cutoff does not prove public release before cutoff. Keep identity repairs and D/R-only source qualification from audit v2.

## Target, refits and priors

Before implementation, explicitly bind the inferential target. The selected `polling_v1.py:147–174` empirical-prior update uses a whole Gaussian poll vector and posterior empirical-row weights; it is a **marginal polling update**, not a calibration cut. Conditional MAP covariance scales add no scale-mixture bank. The separate calibrated M01 bank/consumer work is not silently part of this proposal despite the overloaded M01 label. If scale uncertainty is later integrated, both arms need the same explicit marginal-versus-cut normalization and the same complete joint calibration rows; independently mixing scale marginals is invalid for that joint bank.

Each historical fundamentals bank must be strictly earlier-only, with its complete coefficients, scales and shared latent dependence kept together. Existing complete F0 banks from the inflation experiment are possible reuse candidates, not automatically the selected baseline: verify the exact law, panel/training support, target definition, eligibility and target-cycle prediction operator before reuse. Prefer proof of an existing compatible bank over needless refitting. If the fundamentals law or its inputs change, jointly refit its coefficients/scales; do not fit a new mean and splice it onto old posterior columns. If only the downstream polling covariance changes under a fixed modular bank contract, sharing that exact bank between matched arms is appropriate and does not require arbitrarily refitting upstream fundamentals separately.

The proposed symmetric log-scale regularizer and sensitivities are transparent engineering choices. Their time units and natural-scale implications must be tested over the actual retained time spans. They are not inferred from the 12 selected rises. Label every revision after examined scores as exposed iterative development, as the project verification skill requires; no universal unopened holdout is imposed.

For exact incumbent consistency, call `Normal(log_scale | log_median, .7)` either the smooth penalty on log-SD or the prior density **with respect to d(log SD)** used for log-coordinate MAP. Do not insert the natural-SD lognormal `-log(SD)` Jacobian while continuing to claim the same incumbent log-coordinate objective; MAP depends on coordinates. Conversely, if specifying a density with respect to d(SD), explicitly include that Jacobian and identify the changed objective. Constants irrelevant to fixed-hyperparameter MAP still matter for properly normalized marginal evidence. No marginal-likelihood comparison is needed for the minimal preflight.

The bank availability statement in the proposal is now outdated. The completed `economic-execution-v1/attempt-v1` has independently reviewed earlier-only H0 and F0 raw banks for all four folds: training counts 299/329/357/387, target counts 30/28/30/30, four chains × 4,000 retained joint rows. They are genuine available fits, not a promise to regenerate. H0 has the two-coefficient office/incumbency algebra compatible with `polling_v1.EmpiricalFundamentals` (fixed previous-presidential-logit offset); F0 has the relative-context basis and requires the existing `polling_basis_v1.EmpiricalBasisFundamentals` interface rather than relabeling its six coefficients as two. Neither proves equality to the selected old current-run training panel merely because an interface accepts it. Minimal preflight should bind the chosen H0 or F0 recipe/source-support hashes, reconstruct that fold's target offset/design, export all original joint beta/tau rows without the five already-simulated F-only future replicates, and verify no-poll predictions against the original bank law. If choosing this reviewed historical F0 control, call it that versioned control rather than the old selected two-column prior. No new historical MCMC is necessary solely because the proposal's older intake README said banks were missing.

The planned A2018/2022 and B2020/2024 ordinary Senate comparisons, H14 primary and H30/H7 sensitivities, matched polls/banks/budgets, proper share and winner scores, interval width/coverage and cycle-level reporting are appropriate. Freeze exact score definitions, masks and numerical gates in an executable plan before scoring; do not use noisy near-even realized outcomes to choose the evaluation mask. Numerical MCSE is not election-to-election uncertainty. Examine per-cycle/source/lag losses without requiring every metric in every cycle to improve; a small one-cycle gain cannot establish transfer or complementary mechanism ownership.

Useful next work is the proposed source-pinned no-fit coverage report plus analytic covariance/conditional-forecast tests with the selection and target clarifications above. Only then select a concrete matched runner. The retrospective v2 frequencies remain descriptive: trend and miss share late-poll noise, independent earlier windows still share common errors, field intervals are not publication lags, and four national cycles cannot support causal or broad significance claims. No strongest/weakest-layer ranking, forecast improvement, calibration-cut claim or promotion is supported by this design alone.
