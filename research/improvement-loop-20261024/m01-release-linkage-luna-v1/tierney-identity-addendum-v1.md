# Addendum: Rasmussen-to-538 evidence versus Tierney candidate identity

This addendum narrows the Arizona linkage conclusion in the original packet; it does not change or replace the captured source files or the underlying observations.

## Separate the two claims

1. **Rasmussen primary release ↔ FiveThirtyEight archive 5385932: verified release/event correspondence.** Rasmussen's own October 25, 2012 release and linked question page identify a single-day October 21 Arizona survey of 500 likely voters, the Flake/Carmona Senate question, and results of Carmona 44%, Flake 50%, other 3%, undecided 3%. The pinned 538 row records Arizona Senate, Rasmussen Reports/Pulse Opinion Research, median field date October 21, N=500, and D/R values 44/50. Those particulars substantiate that the 538 record represents the Rasmussen poll event documented in that primary release.
2. **FiveThirtyEight archive 5385932 ↔ prepared Tierney row 3318: strong candidate, not verified identity.** The Tierney row is for the same state, cycle, pollster label, and date string; its author-derived values 220/250 normalize to the release/538 D/R share. This is unusually close content compatibility, but the available evidence does not establish that the Tierney row came from the same respondents, questionnaire, or release. Its row marks population and methodology unknown, publication date blank, source candidate names unverified, sample ID unknown, and source values author-derived. No Tierney source row, lineage, field start, primary question, or sample identifier was checked in this task. The denominator 470 is the sum of the Tierney-derived D/R values and differs from the primary poll's respondent N=500; it is not evidence for a separate N=470 survey, nor proof of a transformation from this release.

## Revised link status

Use this two-part description in any downstream notes: **the 538 entry is verified against Rasmussen's original release; the Tierney association remains a strong but unverified candidate match.** Do not label the Tierney row a verified same-poll event or treat it as an authenticated pair unless its source lineage or other independent sample-level evidence is found. Equal pollster/date and normalized share do not independently identify a sample or instrument.

The poll-event match between publisher release and 538 does not establish that the exact FiveThirtyEight snapshot bytes were available on the Rasmussen release date, and neither relation authenticates the historical byte vintage of the prepared Tierney CSV. The original packet's source captures and HTTP results are preserved unchanged.
