# Independent 2018/2020 CPS replicate-weight follow-up

The historical source gate remains unmet. This is a bounded documentation search, not evidence that November Voting replicate files do not exist. No models, microdata or large datasets were downloaded; no owner/storage configuration was changed. Three small official documentation/index responses and lossy memo text are preserved with exact URL/hash/size/retrieval time in sources.json. Coordinated directly with Luna, whose ASEC/year-page checks remain separate.

The primary Census generic person-supplement memo was independently retrieved at https://www2.census.gov/programs-surveys/cps/datasets/2018/supp/PERSON-level_Use_of_the_Public_Use_Replicate_Weight_File.doc . It is dated March7,2012, rather than a November2018-specific release. Its text instructs a one-to-one QSTNUM/OCCURNUM merge, checking the full-sample PERSUPWGT0 against the person file, and generating160 replicate estimates of the same statistic. It names PWSUPWGT/SRWGT/NRWGT as possible supplement weights and directs researchers to the appropriate research office for replicate files. These generic instructions supply a candidate validation recipe, not an established match for the historical Voting files. The memo's mathematical equations are embedded objects absent from strings extraction; the preserved extraction does not independently validate their exact coefficient.

This distinction matters because Voting technical documentation identifies PWSSWGT as the basic person weight. A similarly named full-weight field or a successful key join cannot prove the correct replicate family. Require an authoritative November/year-specific statement connecting the chosen weight and replicate series, then validate actual key uniqueness, record coverage, numeric precision/implicit scales, full-weight agreement and universe alignment. Do not silently use household, tobacco, October school, ASEC or another month's replicates. Those may have distinct respondents, adjustment/calibration and estimands even where IDs overlap.

The independently fetched official2018/2020 supplement directory listings show their November public files and several other supplement-specific replicate files; they do not list a corresponding November replicate archive in these inspected representations. That is bounded positive evidence about the listings, not a Census declaration of nonavailability. A filename guess, missing page link or absence in one directory cannot justify nonexistence. Luna found primary ASEC replicate resources; ASEC availability establishes neither November Voting availability nor appropriate substitution. No such substitution was made here.

Variance math still needs an applicable source. The commonly described CPS160-replicate rule is Vhat(theta)=4/160 ×sum_r(theta_r−theta)^2. Independent government NTIA documentation describes it, and Census ASEC documentation also describes a160-replicate formula, but neither alone validates a missing November2018/2020 weight family. This review therefore does not authorize that coefficient/family for these unacquired Voting records. Once exact official applicability is established, recompute each ratio/nonlinear estimator under every replicate, and compute joint covariance by the same applicable centered outer-product rule. Replicates are uncertainty calculations, not160 new observations. Preserve shared domain/denominator covariance and singular constraints; do not replace population-weight sums by binomial sample sizes.

For cutoff-valid inference, independently acquire exact November person and compatible replicate bytes/dictionaries, errata/control versions and first-release/revision evidence. A2018 directory's current2020 timestamp or2020 file's2021 timestamp is server metadata, not authenticated first-publication history. A dated table release is not proof of exact raw/replicate availability. Keep event/interview date, first publication, revision and local retrieval/admission separate. Freeze each replay's allowed version before labels; target-cycle post-election files remain outcomes only for that target. Later reweighted/revised files require explicit later-cutoff support.

A defensible next action is documentary clarification from the publisher or an authoritative archived November-specific instruction/file manifest, followed by separately reviewed bounded acquisition and join tests. Actual requests to other people were not sent. Source existence, a join and a variance recipe still would not identify the registration stage skipped for reported voters, account for all self/proxy/nonresponse bias, or establish forecast value. Existing cut/evidence-ownership and matched-replay requirements remain intact.
