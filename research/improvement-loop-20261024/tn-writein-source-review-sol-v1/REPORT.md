# Independent review: Tennessee write-in certificate source

**Decision: ACCEPT the blank form as bounded administrative filing guidance; retain the 2026 named-roster hold.** I recomputed the original [Sumner County PDF](/home/hayden/projects/ballotmancy-improvement-lab/research/improvement-loop-20261024/tn-writein-source-acquisition-luna-v1/raw/sumner-tn-ss3072-blank.pdf) hash and headers. It is a one-page, uncompleted SS-3072 form revised July 2016. Visual inspection confirms its top instruction box separately describes primary and general elections: the Tennessee Coordinator filing route is stated for the listed **primary** offices; for a **general** U.S. Senate write-in, it directs filing in each county making up the district. The signature and election-official receipt lines are blank. It establishes no 2026 person, filing, receipt, or county coverage.

## Independent corroboration and correction

- The [Secretary of State-hosted SS-3072 PDF](https://sos-tn-gov-files.s3.amazonaws.com/Write%20In%20Certificate%202016%20%28SS-3072%29.pdf) returned HTTP 200 on October 25, 2026, and is **byte-for-byte identical** to Luna's Sumner County copy: 88,178 bytes, SHA-256 `86d3b10c1bed02b40e6731beac134ec30c2eed2451b93f696930ec2384550b8e`. The state copy's HTTP `Last-Modified` is May 24, 2017; Sumner's is June 24, 2022. Those are hosting metadata, not proof of a 2026 form revision or what was available at the October 20 owner cutoff. Preserved [state PDF](raw/sos-ss3072.pdf), [headers](raw/sos-ss3072.headers), and [calculation](calculation.json) pin the comparison.
- [Madison County's official write-in guidance](https://madisoncountytn.gov/234/Write-In-Candidates) corroborates the distinction that matters for statewide coverage: a multi-county general-election candidate is directed to file in each county, **but if fewer counties receive a timely certificate, votes are counted only in those filed counties**. A full-district filing and a geographically partial countable filing therefore differ. For statewide U.S. Senate evidence, a single county's blank form cannot establish either a candidate list or county applicability. Complete statewide coverage would require an authoritative 95-county consolidation or sufficiently complete county receipt records. Tennessee's [state county map](https://www.tn.gov/tdot/about/county-outline-map.html) confirms 95 counties. The Madison page is current-hosted guidance with no verified publication/revision date; it is corroboration of the filing distinction, not a candidate-specific legal ruling or a fully verified 2026 codification.
- The form's noon-prevailing-time deadline is the fiftieth day before the election. For November 3, 2026, date arithmetic gives **September 14, 2026**. Passing that date does not establish that nobody filed. The packet's official SOS 2026 candidate-list attempt returned HTTP 403; I verified its 919-byte error body and headers. That response says nothing about the contents or existence of a write-in roster.

## Scope

The packet correctly rejects a zero-filing inference and acknowledges that partial-county filings can yield countable votes in filed counties. Its phrase “must file in each county” should be read as the form's full-district filing direction, **not** as proof that a candidate filing in fewer than all 95 counties has no countable votes anywhere. Neither the 2016-revised blank form nor the undated current-hosted guidance independently settles a particular candidate's legal qualification under 2026 law. No actual 2026 U.S. Senate completed certificate, named consolidated roster, or county receipt set was acquired. October 25 retrieval cannot establish October 20 publication or availability of any actual filing.

No acquisition bytes were altered; no owner, model, input, fit, or source-admission change was made. The [review script](review.py) and [form preview](form-preview.png) are derived verification artifacts.
