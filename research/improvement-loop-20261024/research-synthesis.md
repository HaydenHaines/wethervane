[Fundamentals review](luna-fundamentals-research.md), [participation review](luna-participation-research.md), [complementarity review](sol-complementarity-research.md).

The most immediately specified experiments are M01 and M02 because their omitted uncertainty/bridge is documented in existing code and decisions. That is a readiness judgment, not a performance ranking or instruction to displace the owners' ongoing work. P01/M03 need stronger identification and dated data before implementation. F01 is a bounded feature test. C01/P02 remain scope-dependent candidates. Candidate experience/receipts, broader approval and substate migration stay in the deferred backlog until their dated data and incremental role are clear.

The House owner is separately acquiring and benchmarking historical polls across four fundamentals arms at 14 days in all four cycles. That work is not duplicated here.

The Senate owner independently checked these proposals against the **actual candidate-role pilot**, whose executable lineage differs from the catalog's simple default composition. Its [deduplication note](evidence/senate-owner-deduplication-v1.md) confirms that integrated poll-scale uncertainty, the survey/G bridge, extra registration/validation stages, dated state income and residual joint-ballot parameters are absent there. Shared event G already enters the **EAVS administrative-report intensity**; M02 concerns only the survey-reported-turnout probability. Exact coordinate/worker-loading repairs add none of these mechanisms. The owner retains those repairs and actual fitting; the latest startup progress is not forecast qualification.

## Evidence and corrections that changed the proposals

**Polling uncertainty:** the local `research/polling-layer-implementation-20261020/final-decision-root-v2.md` explicitly states that current aggregate forecasts condition on fitted scales and remain sensitive to them. The review also retains actual score regressions and unresolved challenger differences. This motivates M01 but does not prove it wins. A primary discussion of a different forecasting system identifies covariance and nonsampling-error estimation as consequential choices; it is supporting methodology, not Ballotmancy evidence. [Gelman, Goodrich and Han](https://doi.org/10.1162/99608f92.a919e3fa).

**Turnout bridge:** selected `components.py` uses `sigmoid(turnout_logit + source_turnout)`; `design-astra-v1/design.md` already proposes a separately fitted `log G_e` alternative. `G_e` scales event counts, so `q G_e` must not be casually interpreted as a bounded Bernoulli probability. This is an existing untested proposal, not a newly discovered missing turnout layer.

**Registration data:** EAC provides a harmonized 2004–2024 EAVS archive, including registration and participation material; Census supplies CPS voting supplements for all four A/B cycles. The modern archive is an acquisition route, not proof that those exact bytes existed at an earlier forecast cutoff. [EAC](https://www.eac.gov/research-and-data/eavs-retrospective), [Census](https://www.census.gov/data/developers/data-sets/census-microdata-api/cps/voting.html).

**Jurisdiction exception:** North Dakota's official election guidance says voter registration is not required. Root caught this after both Luna proposals initially described a generic registration hurdle. Any national alternative needs a separate supported regime there and date-specific rules elsewhere; zero registered-voter data is not zero possible voters. [North Dakota Secretary of State](https://www.sos.nd.gov/elections/voter/voting-north-dakota/forms-voter-id), [dated 2024 guidance](https://www.sos.nd.gov/sites/www/files/documents/news/2024-general-election-media-info-oct24.pdf).

**Survey weighting:** normalized release laws, respondent composition models and weighted pseudo-posteriors answer different questions. Existing implementations must be compared before adding another selection correction. Weighted pseudo-posteriors can be valid under explicitly stated sampling assumptions; they are not universally invalid. They cannot silently replace this graph's likelihood while claiming the same target and protected normalization. [Savitsky and Toth](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07050).

**Economic data:** the legacy `unemployment_oct` is national FRED `UNRATE`, not state unemployment. An initial Luna inference from state release dates was retracted. The local historical table contains midterms only, so it does not currently contain a leaked 2020 row. A future 2020 port could not use October national unemployment in an Election Day forecast: that report was released November 6. Every horizon needs its own availability check, including revisions. [BLS archive](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_11062020.htm).

**Economics literature:** state personal-income growth has appeared in published Senate forecasting models. This supplies a concrete prior hypothesis, not a guarantee for the current stack, era or target. The more expansive macro/approval bundle is already a legacy implementation and has substantial overlap with common political conditions. [Hummel and Rothschild](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0261379414000602).

**Joint ballots:** a public 2020 cast-vote-record database demonstrates available individual ballot patterns across selected jurisdictions. Its paper was published November 28, 2024, after the 2024 election; earlier usable release versions need independent authentication before a 2024 replay. The sample is not a representative national electorate. [Kuriwaki and colleagues](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-024-04017-1).

## What the next round must resolve

Obtain a qualified full-count reference and matched component evidence from the Senate owner. Bind any independent experiment to an explicit scope and baseline rather than claiming to improve an unfitted reference. For each promoted candidate, require a preregistered-by-version comparison specification, source/cutoff admission, full joint refits, numerical checks, proper predictive scores, A/B transfer evidence and independent review.

Keep other component **definitions** fixed for a one-component comparison, not their fitted parameters. A/B cycles are revisitable development benchmarks under the project skill; “held out” means target labels do not train their own replay, not an invented sealed-holdout policy. Adoption need not win every metric in every election. Preserve practical gains, losses, uncertainty and all negative decisions.

No models were fitted, promoted or published by this research pass. The goal, evidence archive, protocol and journal preparation are concrete progress toward the repeated loop.
