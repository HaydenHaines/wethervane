# Independent registration/participation challenge

Source-only review plus one exact-rational invented test; no fits, source modifications or owner jobs. Exact inspected file hashes are in source-pins.json. Luna described a feasibility-first CPS design-weighted joint registration/voting outcome, with EAVS validation/alternate margin and a jointly refit matched replacement. No admitted CPS adapter or empirical benefit has been demonstrated by this review.

The selected graph already has demographic/community/state/cycle turnout, an event Gamma multiplier, contest response and survey/report measurement. Participation computes q=sigmoid(turnout_logit) and exposure×community×q. Measurement separately computes survey h=sigmoid(turnout_logit+source_turnout), and one NegativeBinomial factor per participation report owns report:<id>. Thus a Participation-only hurdle with q=r×v cannot leave turnout_logit as either stage. It must expose the compatible marginal logit(q) while preserving semantics, or explicitly version the measurement interface. Adding registration observations also requires an explicit admitted data/measurement contract: current strict survey rows admit only record_id, cell_id, community_mask, turnout_mask, answer_mask. They contain no registration axis, design weights or replicate covariance. Weighting these existing raw-record log factors is not automatically a normalized CPS observation law.

The runnable check_identification.py uses invented values (r,v)=(3/4,4/5) and (4/5,3/4). Both give q=3/5 and exactly identical eleven Binomial(10,q) masses. It proves an elementary factorization nonidentification, not equivalence of every possible extended graph. Existing marginal turnout observations cannot distinguish these stages absent compatible registration information; new priors can choose one stage without data identifying it. Source offsets and reporting error add further plausible confounding. Administrative registration and CPS self-report are not interchangeable truth labels.

Data availability is narrower than an online source list. The inspected selected Problem input has no registration field. Its admitted sources require available_utc<=cutoff; reports are restricted to historical events and release-after-event support. Any new adapter must retain those checks and pin exact historical releases. The existing Florida county feature module is a different object: latest snapshot plus earliest-to-latest growth over every supplied year, with no cutoff argument. Feeding the full 2016–2024 table into a 2018 replay would include future snapshots. Pre-filter by actually available release, preserve vintage, and test future-row rejection; do not treat this FL-only partisan registration feature table as national demographic registration/voting observations.

Previous evidence is not a blank slate. The report benchmark compared 2020→2024 EAVS F1a carry-forward against resident growth, and carry won its three point losses. Its README explicitly calls this one temporal transition and exposed development evidence, not 51 independent cycle replications or uncertainty qualification. The S186 turnout-dimension clustering report concerns county shift KMeans holdout correlation; it does not identify a registration hurdle or qualify a Senate candidate-count improvement. Its broad 'optimal' wording is limited to its tested variants/metric, not a theorem against a future participation challenger.

A falsifiable next test is an adapter-and-identification gate before fitting: on a frozen historical CPS source, publish the joint R/V state definitions, missing/not-in-universe handling, age/citizenship/eligibility universe, state/demographic crosswalk and full design/replicate uncertainty construction. Demonstrate whether registration supplies information beyond voting after measurement bias allowances. Include the equal-product counterexample as a negative control: without registration observations the proposed stage parameters must not be reported identified. Handle nonregistration/same-day-registration regimes explicitly; CVAP is exposure, not exact legal eligibility. No numerical jurisdiction-law claim was independently checked here.

Only after that gate, predeclare matched whole-stack historical replays: same source roster/cutoffs/population/choice/count target; replace the participation mechanism and any genuinely required compatible measurement contract, and jointly refit all parameters. Keep source identity ownership unique: never enter the same EAVS administrative total as both old report likelihood and new stage-count likelihood, nor raw CPS respondents plus their deterministic margins as independent evidence. Compare original-row joint count/event-total proper scores and calibration by election (2018/2022 midterms separately from 2020/2024 presidential). Target-cycle post-election CPS/EAVS are scoring labels only, or later-cycle training after actual release; no revised/current file substitution. Previously inspected 2024 EAVS is exposed development evidence. Predeclare material-regression guards and report every cycle/missing-support case; no claim of fresh confirmation from reusing exposed outcomes.

The proposed feasibility research is defensible with those interface and identification gates. It is not yet an implemented one-provider drop-in, an admitted national dataset, or a forecast improvement.
